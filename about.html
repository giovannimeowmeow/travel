About WANDERLUST

Group Name: Wanderlust Crew

Wanderlust is a student-led travel website created for a web development assignment. The project was designed to showcase travel journeys, destination inspiration, media content and interactive user experience.

Our goal is to help visitors discover beautiful destinations, understand journey planning, and enjoy a polished digital travel experience.
